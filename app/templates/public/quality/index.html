6-Stage Quality Assurance Lifecycle

GATE 01
Raw Material Receipt

Optical emission spectrometer verification, spark test, and ultrasonic internal soundness scan on raw ingots.

GATE 02
Hot Forging In-Process

Pyrometer optical thermal monitoring, grain flow verification, and dimensional gauge inspection during forging.

GATE 03
Heat Treatment Validation

Continuous SCADA thermocouple logging, Brinell/Rockwell hardness mapping, and tensile coupon testing.

GATE 04
NDT & Metallography

Immersion ultrasonic testing, magnetic particle crack detection, and microstructure optical microscopy.

GATE 05
Precision Metrology

Multi-axis coordinate measuring machine (CMM) verification, surface roughness (Ra) profiling, and geometric tolerances.

GATE 06
Dispatch & 3.1 MTR

Dot-peen laser marking with heat number, protective anti-corrosion coating, and EN 10204 3.1 inspection certification.

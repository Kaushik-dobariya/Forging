END-TO-END EXECUTION

Our Manufacturing Process

From 3D DEFORM metal flow simulations to ultrasonic inspection and robotic dispatch.

STEP 01

Die Design & FEA Simulation

Computer-modeled grain orientation preventing internal shear stresses and flash wastage.

STEP 02

Induction Billet Heating

Continuous optical pyrometry ensuring strict thermal window control to prevent grain boundary burning.

STEP 03

High-Energy Hot Forging

Multi-strike press deformation delivering high grain density and superior tensile integrity.

STEP 04

Controlled Heat Treatment

Automated normalizing, quenching, and tempering achieving specified metallurgical hardness.

STEP 05

Precision CNC Machining

Vertical CNC machining centers delivering assembly-ready components within ±0.015 mm tolerances.

STEP 06

Spectrometry & NDT Testing

100% volumetric ultrasonic testing, wet MPI, and EN 10204 3.1 inspection certification.

ZERO DEFECT RIGOR

Certified Metallurgy & Volumetric NDT Testing

Our in-house NABL certified metallurgical laboratory operates 24-channel optical emission spectrometers, automated Brinell hardness testing, and Charpy impact testing down to -40°C.

Every heat lot is accompanied by complete chemical composition and mechanical test certificates meeting EN 10204 3.1 requirements.

ENGINEERING RIGOR

Science-Led Metallurgy & Continuous Grain Alignment

Unlike casting which suffers from internal porosity and dendritic segregation, hot forging refines the crystalline microstructure. Our engineering team utilizes finite element DEFORM simulation to model metal flow inside closed dies prior to cutting tool steel.

Every production batch undergoes strict pre-heating window control via infrared pyrometry, automated descaling to prevent oxide inclusions, and dedicated post-forge thermal cycle controls.

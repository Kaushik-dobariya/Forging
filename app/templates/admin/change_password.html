Password Security Policy

To safeguard enterprise forging equipment records and engineering specifications, your password must satisfy all of the following rules:

  • At least 8 characters in length
  • At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  • At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • At least one digit or special symbol (0-9, @, #, $, etc.)
  • Cannot reuse your current password
Session Invalidation Notice
